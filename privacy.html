Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version: Morpho processes your files entirely on your device. We do not upload, collect, store, or sell your files or personal information. The tools work offline.

Who we are

Morpho ("the app", "we", "us") is a file-tools application for Android. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Files you process

All file processing in Morpho's offline tools — including PDF, image, audio, and video operations — happens locally on your device. Your files are never uploaded to us or to any server for these tools. When you save output, it is written to your device's storage (such as your Downloads or gallery) at your request. We never see, transmit, or retain your files.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account, sign-in, or registration.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or contacts.
  • We do not access your files except the specific file you choose for a tool, and only on your device.
  • We do not track your location.

Advertising

The free version of Morpho displays ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google's advertising services may collect and use certain information, such as a device advertising identifier, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. This is standard for ad-supported apps.

  • You can review how Google uses this information at Google's Privacy & Terms.
  • Where required by law (such as in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), Morpho presents a consent form so you can choose how your data is used for ads. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings.
  • Morpho Plus subscribers do not see ads, and the advertising SDK does not request ads for them.

Morpho Plus subscriptions

If you purchase a Morpho Plus subscription, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google processes payment information under its own terms.

Permissions

Morpho requests only the permissions needed for its tools to function, such as access to a file you select, or to save output to your device. Internet access is used to load ads and, in the future, optional cloud features. We do not use permissions to gather data about you.

Children

Morpho is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
